   - From: `` `${skill.description}\nCommonly useful in careers and education paths` ``
   - To: `` `${skill.description} — Commonly useful in careers and education paths` ``

This produces a readable horizontal tooltip like: "Skill description — Commonly useful in careers and education paths" on one or two natural-width lines.
